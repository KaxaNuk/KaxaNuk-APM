## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-09
### Added
- `data-analyzer-signal-screening`: the `Data/analyzer.ipynb` contract of a KN Research Process
  repository — coverage before conclusions, what diversification is available, the rank identity,
  the per-date information coefficient and ratio over the whole panel and over the eligible pool,
  persistence as the turnover floor, the two questions any signal owes, and what the stage licenses
  a blueprint to predict. Written from the template's 0.6.0 contract; the worked example is the
  `example` branch's jump model.
